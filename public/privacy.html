Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

DeliverIQ is a private email outreach tool operated by Haitham Soud. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use DeliverIQ.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • Account password (stored securely using bcrypt hashing)
  • Workspace information

Google Account Access

When you connect your Gmail account via Google OAuth, we request access to the following Google API scopes:

  • gmail.send - To send campaign emails on your behalf using your Gmail account
  • gmail.readonly - To detect bounced emails and delivery status notifications in your inbox
  • userinfo.email - To identify which Gmail account is connected

We store your Gmail email address and an encrypted OAuth refresh token. We do not store your Gmail password.

Campaign and Contact Data

We store the following data you provide:

  • Contact lists (recipient names, email addresses, timezones, notes)
  • Email templates and campaign content
  • Campaign scheduling and delivery information
  • Email engagement data (opens, clicks, bounces, unsubscribes)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide DeliverIQ's email campaign services:

  • Send campaign emails through your connected Gmail account
  • Check your Gmail inbox for bounce notifications and delivery failures
  • Track email opens and clicks for campaign analytics
  • Manage your contact lists and campaigns
  • Process unsubscribe requests

3. Google API Services User Data Policy

DeliverIQ's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • Gmail data is used only to send your campaign emails and detect bounces
  • Gmail data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising purposes
  • Gmail data is not used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models
  • Access to Gmail is limited to the specific scopes described above
  • Human review of Gmail data only occurs for debugging or support at your explicit request

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, except:

  • Service Providers: We use Neon (PostgreSQL database), Vercel (hosting), and Upstash (Redis) to operate DeliverIQ. These providers are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

Your emails are sent directly through Google's Gmail API. Google's own privacy policy applies to their handling of email transmission.

5. Email Tracking

DeliverIQ includes email tracking features:

  • Open Tracking: A small 1x1 pixel image is embedded in HTML emails to detect when they are opened
  • Click Tracking: Links in emails are rewritten to pass through our tracking system before redirecting to the destination
  • Unsubscribe: Each email includes an unsubscribe link. Recipients who unsubscribe are marked in your contact list and will not receive future campaigns

These tracking features are standard email marketing practices and help you measure campaign effectiveness.

6. Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your data:

  • Gmail OAuth refresh tokens are encrypted using AES-256-CBC before storage
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with salt
  • All connections use HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Database access is restricted and authenticated

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Automatic Deletion

  • When you disconnect Gmail in Settings, your encrypted OAuth tokens are immediately deleted from our database
  • Google automatically revokes our access to your Gmail when you remove DeliverIQ from your Google account permissions

Account Deletion

To delete your account and all associated data (contacts, campaigns, templates, engagement history), contact us at haithamsoud822@gmail.com. We will permanently delete your data within 30 days.

Data Retention

We retain your campaign and contact data for as long as your account is active. Bounce and engagement data is retained to maintain campaign history and analytics.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights:

  • Access: You can view all your data within the DeliverIQ application
  • Correction: You can edit your account, contact, and campaign data at any time
  • Deletion: You can disconnect Gmail, delete contacts, campaigns, and templates, or request full account deletion
  • Revocation: You can revoke DeliverIQ's access to Gmail at any time through your Google Account settings

9. Cookies and Local Storage

DeliverIQ uses:

  • Authentication Cookie: A session cookie to keep you logged in
  • Local Storage: Browser local storage may cache application state for performance

These are essential for the application to function and are not used for tracking or advertising.

10. Third-Party Services

DeliverIQ integrates with:

  • Google Gmail API: For sending emails and reading bounce notifications (covered by Google's Privacy Policy)
  • SendGrid (optional): If configured, may be used as an alternative email provider (covered by SendGrid's Privacy Policy)

11. Children's Privacy

DeliverIQ is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
